      <h3 align="center">🎴 When They Cry Social &nbsp;·&nbsp; <sub>umineko_city_of_books</sub></h3>
      <p align="center">

        The original goal was a place to declare fan theories as <b><a href="https://whentheycry.social/theories/umineko">blue truth</a></b>, attach quotes from the game as evidence,<br/>

      </p>
      <p align="center">
        It has since grown into a full social platform: theory debates across all three series, a Twitter-style game board, mystery boards,<br/>
        fan art galleries, ship and OC declarations, fanfiction, live reading journals, chat rooms with shared-browser watch parties,<br/>
        DMs, secret unlock hunts, multiplayer games with their own vanity titles, site-wide search, live notifications, and themed role-based moderation.
